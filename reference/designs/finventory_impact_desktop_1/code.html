Waste Calculation Standard expand_more
Our waste redirection is calculated using the NOAA Seafood Waste Metrics v2.4. We measure the difference between potential landfill contribution and actual redistribution through the Finventory network, accounting for shipping distance and packaging decay factors.
Carbon Offset Validation expand_more
Carbon avoidance is audited annually by independent third-party climate scientists. We use life-cycle analysis (LCA) to determine the net reduction in methane and transport-related CO2 across our logistics layer.
